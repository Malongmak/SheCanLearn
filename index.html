Mission & Vision

SheCanLearn is committed to ensuring that African refugee school girls have the tools, confidence, and support they need to shape their own futures. We co-create safe spaces in camps and host communities where digital learning, life skills, and sexuality education meet safeguarding and joy.

We envision a world where every refugee girl can stay in school, make safe, informed choices about her health, and lead community change. Our approach is trauma-informed, youth-led, and rooted in partnerships with refugee-led groups, schools, and humanitarian organizations.

What we run

Programs for resilient learning

Digital Learning Hubs

Offline-friendly, solar-powered spaces that keep girls learning even when schools are disrupted. Devices are preloaded with localized curriculum, STEM playlists, and safeguarding tips.

  • Offline-first playlists & micro-learning
  • Guardianship protocols & safe transport
  • Solar + battery backups for reliability

Financial Literacy & Life Skills

Budgeting, savings groups, basic entrepreneurship, and everyday problem-solving for girls and their caregivers, delivered in interactive circles.

  • Youth-friendly cash flow simulations
  • Intergenerational money dialogues
  • Micro-grants for girl-led projects

Leadership & Mentorship

Confidence-building, public speaking, and peer mentorship for refugee girls. Alumni co-facilitate sessions and are supported to launch community campaigns.

  • Youth councils & storytelling labs
  • Mentorship badges and pathways
  • Shadowing humanitarian professionals

Sexuality & Health Education

Age-appropriate lessons on body safety, consent, healthy relationships, and reproductive health—co-designed with parents, teachers, and safeguarding officers.

  • Consent-focused storytelling
  • Survivor-centered referral routes
  • Male ally & caregiver workshops

About SheCanLearn

Who we are: SheCanLearn is a nonprofit initiative created by educators and community organizers who have worked alongside refugee communities for many years. We saw too many girls forced out of school by displacement, early marriage, and lack of safe spaces—and decided to act.

We partner with local schools, refugee-led groups, and humanitarian organizations to co-design programs that are practical, culturally relevant, and rooted in girls’ everyday realities. Our work focuses on access to learning, safety and wellbeing, and leadership so girls can influence decisions that affect their lives.

Three pillars

  • Access to inclusive learning powered by low-data tech, mentors, and safe spaces.
  • Safety & wellbeing through safeguarding, psychosocial care, and trusted adults.
  • Leadership opportunities for girls to co-create solutions and influence policy.
